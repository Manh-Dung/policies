Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 13, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how FitCoach AI: Workout Tracker ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing a transparent, secure environment for your fitness journey.

2. Data Collection and Usage

We believe your personal fitness information belongs to you. All core fitness data you enter into the application — including your fitness profile (gender, age, weight, height, goal, experience level, available equipment), workout routines, exercise logs, sets, reps, RPE, body measurements, and custom routines — is stored locally on your device.

We do not transmit, sell, or store your personal workout history on our private servers. You have complete autonomy and control over your data.

When you use the AI workout generation feature, an anonymized summary of your goal, experience level, and available equipment is securely sent to our AI backend to create customized training plans. No personally identifiable information (such as your name, email, or device identifiers) is sent with these plan requests.

3. Permissions

To deliver an optimal workout and tracking experience, the application may request the following device permissions:

Permission Purpose
Notifications To send workout reminders, rest timer alerts, and recovery schedule notifications.
Alarms & Precise Timers To ensure workout interval countdowns and rest timers trigger accurately between sets.
Internet Access To stream exercise demonstration media from public content distribution networks, generate AI training plans, process in-app purchases, and report crash analytics.

4. Third-Party Services

We use a limited number of trusted third-party SDKs for application stability, performance diagnostics, advertisements, and store payments. We do not sell your personal data. These services may collect pseudonymous diagnostic or device information according to their respective policies:

  • Firebase Analytics: To understand aggregated user trends and optimize app performance. Firebase Analytics Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: To collect crash reports and fix technical issues. Firebase Crashlytics Terms & Privacy.
  • Google AdMob: To display contextual advertisements. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play In-App Billing: To securely process subscriptions and in-app purchases. Handled directly by Google Play.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Your fitness logs, custom exercises, routines, and profile metrics are stored locally on your device and are not stored on our remote servers. You can permanently delete all your data at any time by:

  • Clearing the app's cache and data in your device's Settings > Apps > FitCoach AI > Storage > Clear Data, or
  • Uninstalling the application from your device.

For automatically collected diagnostic and analytics data via Firebase, data is pseudonymous, aggregated, and retained for up to 14 months before automated deletion.

If you have contacted us directly via email (e.g., for technical support) and wish to request deletion of your communication records, please contact us at manhdung5a@gmail.com. We will fulfill your request within 30 business days.

6. Children's Privacy

Our service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top.
