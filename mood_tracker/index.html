Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 20, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Diary with Lock Password ("we," "us," or "our") handles information. We are committed to protecting your privacy, especially regarding your personal mood and journal data, and ensuring a secure experience.

2. Data Collection and Usage

We respect your privacy. Core data you enter into the application — such as mood entries, journal notes, daily activities, and personal goals — is stored locally on your device and is not sent to any external servers. You have full control over your personal information.

We may automatically collect certain non-personal, aggregated data through third-party analytics services to help us understand general app usage patterns, diagnose technical issues, and improve the overall user experience.

3. Permissions

To provide a seamless mood tracking and journaling experience, the app may request the following permissions:

Permission Purpose
Notifications To send timely reminders for daily mood check-ins and journaling.
Alarms & Timers To ensure notifications and reminders are triggered accurately on time.
Media / Storage To allow you to attach images to your journal entries and mood records.
Internet Access To facilitate anonymous analytics, error reporting, display advertisements, and handle in-app purchases.

4. Third-Party Services

We use a limited number of third-party services for app stability, analytics, and advertisements. We do not sell your personal data.

  • Firebase Analytics: To understand general app usage trends and improve user experience. Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: To identify and fix technical issues and app crashes. Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob: To serve advertisements. Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Services: Required for core Android OS functionalities.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Your core app data (mood entries, journal notes, activities) is stored locally on your device and is not sent to any external servers. You can permanently delete all your data at any time by uninstalling the application from your device or clearing the app's data in your device's settings.

For automatically collected information via third-party services (like Firebase Analytics), data is largely aggregated and pseudonymous. We retain such information for up to 14 months. You can stop all collection of information by uninstalling the Application.

If you have provided us with any direct personal information (like an email address for support) and wish to have it deleted, please contact us at manhdung5a@gmail.com. We will process your request and delete your data within a reasonable timeframe.

6. Children's Privacy

Our service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
