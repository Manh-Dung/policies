Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 26, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Quit Smoking App Free ("we," "us," or "our") handles information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a secure experience while you build healthy habits and organize your daily routines.

2. Data Collection and Usage

We respect your privacy. Core data you enter into the application — including habits, daily routines, progress records, streaks, and personal goals — is stored locally on your device and is not sent to any external servers. You have full control over your personal data.

We may automatically collect certain non-personal, aggregated data through third-party services to help us understand general app usage patterns, diagnose technical issues, and improve the overall user experience.

3. Permissions

To provide an effective habit tracking and reminder experience, the app may request the following permissions:

Permission Purpose
Notifications To send timely reminders for your scheduled habits and routines.
Alarms & Timers To ensure reminders and notifications are triggered accurately at scheduled times.
Internet Access To facilitate anonymous analytics, crash reporting, display advertisements, and handle in-app purchases.

4. Third-Party Services

We use a limited number of third-party services for app stability, analytics, and advertisements. We do not sell your personal data.

  • Firebase Analytics: To understand general app usage trends and improve user experience. Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: To identify and fix technical issues and app crashes. Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob: To serve advertisements. Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Services: Required for core Android OS functionalities and Google Play In-App Billing.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Your core app data (habits, schedules, check-in history, notes) is stored locally on your device and is not sent to any external servers. You can permanently delete all your data at any time by uninstalling the application from your device or clearing the app's data in your device's settings.

For automatically collected information via third-party services (like Firebase Analytics), data is largely aggregated and pseudonymous. We retain such information for up to 14 months. You can stop all collection of information by uninstalling the Application.

If you have provided us with any direct personal information (like an email address for support) and wish to have it deleted, please contact us at manhdung5a@gmail.com. We will process your request and delete your data within a reasonable timeframe.

6. Children's Privacy

Our service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
