Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 20, 2026

Important Disclaimer: Retro Emulator – Multi System is an independent emulator frontend application. It does not bundle, host, distribute, or provide any game ROMs, BIOS files, or copyrighted material. Users are solely responsible for obtaining and supplying their own legally acquired game backups from their personal physical media.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Retro Emulator – Multi System ("we," "us," or "our") handles your information. We prioritize user privacy and adhere to an offline-first architecture. We do not require account registration, do not track personal identities, and do not transmit your game files, save states, or personal data to external servers.

2. Data Collection and Storage

Our application is designed to operate locally on your device:

  • Game Files & Save Data: Your game ROMs, in-game save files (.srm, .sav), save states, game configuration bindings, and favorites lists are stored strictly on your local device storage or SD card. We do not transmit or store your game data on external servers.
  • No User Account / Registration: You are not required to create an account, log in, or provide any personal details to use the application.
  • Usage & Analytics: The application does not collect personal identity information, phone numbers, or contacts.

3. Device Permissions and Usage

To deliver emulator functionality, controller support, and game management, the application may request the following permissions:

Permission Purpose
Storage Access / SAF (Storage Access Framework) Allows you to select a specific folder on your device containing your ROMs. The app reads game files and writes in-game saves and save states locally to your chosen directory.
Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) Optional permission required solely for emulated systems (such as Nintendo DS) that support microphone interaction (e.g., blowing into the mic in specific games). Audio is processed in real time purely for game input simulation; no audio is ever recorded, stored, or transmitted anywhere.
Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) Used on Android 13+ to display ongoing notifications while an emulation session is running in the foreground or when local storage scanning is in progress.
Internet Access Used strictly for downloading public game metadata, cover art, and box art from open databases (such as Libretro DB), and for standard Android OS services. No personal information is transmitted.
Bluetooth & Gamepad Controllers Used to detect and map physical game controllers, Bluetooth gamepads, and USB input devices for in-game control.
Vibration / Haptics Provides haptic feedback for on-screen touch controls and rumble effects for supported controllers.

4. Third-Party Services

The application relies on minimal core platform services:

  • Google Play Services: Required for fundamental Android platform features, app distribution, and updates. Google Privacy Policy.

We do not use third-party advertising networks, third-party analytics trackers, or data broker SDKs. We do not sell, rent, or trade your data.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all core data (games, saves, preferences, states, and recent history) is stored exclusively on your device, you have complete control over your data at all times:

  • Deleting App Data: You can delete all application settings and cached data at any time via your device settings (Settings > Apps > Retro Emulator > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data).
  • Deleting Game & Save Files: You can access your chosen ROM folder using any file manager on your device to inspect, backup, or permanently delete any save files or game directories.
  • Uninstalling: Uninstalling the application will remove the app and its internal application sandbox files from your device.
  • User Data Deletion Inquiries: If you have contacted us via email (e.g., for customer support or feedback) and wish to have your communication history deleted, please email us at manhdung5a@gmail.com. We will fulfill your request promptly within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

Our application is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. Since the app functions offline without user accounts or data collection, no personal data from any user, including children, is gathered or stored externally.

7. Security

We value your trust in using our application. Since the application does not transmit user data across external networks or store it in remote databases, the risk of external data breach is minimized. All game states and configurations remain within your device's secured local sandbox and user-approved storage paths.
